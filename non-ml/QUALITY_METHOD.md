# Facility assessment and ranking

The balanced ranking favors better-documented options within the same service evidence tier. The published material in this snapshot supports checks for corroboration and assessment scope. Applying a clinical quality rating would require comparable measurements of care or outcomes.

## How facilities are assessed

| Standard | What assessors examine | What the project can use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| India’s National Quality Assurance Standards (NQAS) | Eight areas cover service provision, patient rights, inputs, support, clinical care, infection control, quality management and outcomes. National certification uses a 70% aggregate threshold, department and area thresholds, and checks on individual standards. Conditional certification follows a different rule. | A full, unexpired certificate linked to the exact public facility gives facility-level assessment evidence. The directory lists a status and date. [NQAS standards](https://qps.nhsrcindia.org/national-quality-assurance-standards) · [Certification criteria, July 2025](https://qps.nhsrcindia.org/sites/default/files/2025-07/DO%20letter%20to%20states%207july%202025%20NQAS%26Kayakalp.pdf) |
| NABH hospital accreditation | Assessors review documents, carry out onsite assessments and review corrective actions. Its sixth-edition final assessment uses at least 80% overall compliance plus specific requirements for objective elements, standards and chapters. | A current, reviewed scope certificate that names the same branch and requested service adds assessment evidence. The certificate's program and edition matter. The thresholds above describe accreditation decisions. [NABH hospital program](https://nabh.co/programmes/hospitals-accreditation-programme-hco/) · [Sixth-edition standards, pp. 13–16](https://portal.nabh.co/images/Standards/NABH%20Hospital%20Accreditation%20Standard%206th%20Edition%20January%202025.pdf) |
| WHO Harmonized Health Facility Assessment (HHFA) | Separate modules examine whether a service exists, readiness to deliver it, quality and safety of care, and management. Readiness needs observations of staff, equipment, supplies and other inputs. Care quality draws on patient records. | These distinctions guide the evidence fields. The project lacks comparable facility-level readiness observations, patient experience measures and clinical outcomes. [WHO HHFA introduction](https://www.who.int/data/data-collection-tools/harmonized-health-facility-assessment/introduction) |

A facility-record study in selected Indian districts found that 3 of 13 comprehensive-designated facilities documented all nine assessed emergency obstetric and newborn functions in the study period. A separate rural Gujarat study found that patients bypassed less functional obstetric facilities. These findings support looking at the specific capability a patient needs, alongside travel. A safe detour and present clinical performance remain unmeasured here. [Obstetric facility-record study](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0287904) · [Gujarat bypassing study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4992671/)

## How the recommendation uses this

1. **Require the requested service and a routable branch.** A candidate needs direct evidence of the exact service at the correct branch and a usable site point. An applicable contradiction keeps it out of recommendations. Accreditation never establishes an unlisted service.
2. **Prepare two documented signals.** Notebook 04c records independent corroboration for the specific service and a current external assessment. It keeps quotes, source links, scope, dates and the status of each credential. Unlinked, expired, conditional and undated credentials stay visible. Missing credentials remain unknown.
3. **Combine travel and documentation within each evidence tier.** Start with the selected route distance or time. Subtract up to **5 km** or **10 minutes** of ranking credit, using the same units across compared options. Independent service corroboration earns 60% of that budget and a current assessment earns 40%. Shorter travel wins a tie. The displayed route distance and travel time stay unchanged. These limits and shares are project defaults for demonstration, with no clinical validation. [Ranking configuration](config/project.json)
4. **Show the trade-off.** Notebook 07 provides the travel-only rank, balanced rank, evidence behind each credit, and the ranking basis. Set `FACILITY_SORT = "closest"` to order by travel within the same evidence rules. Emergency and trauma searches stay travel-first. Load balancing remains opt-in and applies its separate detour limit relative to the ordinary recommendation. [Recommendation notebook](notebooks/07_compare_and_feedback.ipynb)

The current snapshot ranks with independent documentation and proximity. Facility size, imputed beds or staffing, review volume and an unreviewed IVR answer carry no assessment credit. For future clinical-quality comparisons, collect the same service-level measure for each facility, with comparable reporting periods, denominators and case-mix information. Check coverage and rank changes by geography and facility type before broadening the policy.

The real snapshot yielded three branch-service profiles with a dated, service-scoped NABH certificate. One is Santokba Durlabhji Memorial Hospital Campus in Jaipur, where the hospital's active accreditation scope lists its blood bank. That corroborates the branch-specific directory entry and earns documentation credit in the lookup. The evidence supports the accredited scope. Current blood inventory and clinical outcomes remain unknown. The 12,440 full, unexpired NQAS directory rows had no exact link to a routable branch in the prepared data. ## Where to find patient ratings and reviews

| Source | What to look for | Use in the project |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mera Aspataal** | Government patient satisfaction reports for participating facilities. The NHM program describes standardized facility identifiers. Its reporting dashboard serves authorized stakeholders, and some hospitals publish their own aggregate reports. | Request an authorized branch-level export with NIN, reporting window, question and response count, then establish the crosswalk to the facility dataset. [NHM program guide](https://nhm.gov.in/uhc-day/Session%202/eBooklet%20on%20IT%20Initiatives%20of%20NHM.pdf) · [Report instructions](https://qps.nhsrcindia.org/sites/default/files/2022-04/Process%20Note-%20Mera%20Aspataal%20Calculation%20under%20Kayakalp%20%202019-20.pdf) |
| **PM-JAY beneficiary feedback** | Post-discharge patient experience for participating scheme facilities, with a scheme-specific Hospital ID. | Seek approved facility-level access through the program. Current public dashboard coverage has no verified downloadable branch-level satisfaction feed. Preserve scheme and patient cohort in any comparison. [NHA annual report](https://nha.gov.in/img/resources/Annual-Report-2020-21.pdf) · [PM-JAY insights](https://insights.pmjay.gov.in/) |
| **Google Maps and Practo** | Look up the exact branch by name and published address. Google Places can supply a star average and rating count; Practo’s patient stories may describe a doctor inside the branch. | Show a branch search link. Consider on-demand, attributed display via an approved integration. Obtain terms that permit storage and ranking before adding any platform aggregate to the Databricks snapshot. Google restricts storage and derived use. Practo restricts automated copying and scopes its partner API to the agreement. [Google Places fields](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/place-details) · [Google Places policies](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/policies) · [Practo Soni branch listing](https://www.practo.com/jaipur/hospital/soni-hospital-4-markhi) · [Practo terms](https://www.practo.com/company/terms) |

Safdarjung Hospital's public Mera Aspataal report shows 14,605 responses, with 70.1% satisfied or very satisfied. About 0.92% of visits with phone numbers produced a response. The report prints a period of December 29, 2024 through January 3, 2026. The matching project listing is for the hospital's blood bank, so the hospital-wide satisfaction figure remains unattached. This example illustrates the importance of scope, denominators and response patterns. [Safdarjung report, page 1](https://vmmc-sjh.mohfw.gov.in/sites/default/files/Mera%20Aspataal%20Report%20-%20Year%202025%20%28January%20to%20December%202025%29.pdf#page=1)

Notebook 04c can read a permitted CSV using `DATATHON_PATIENT_RATINGS`. Provide one aggregate per exact `facility_id`, along with `source`, `source_url`, `metric`, `period_start`, `period_end`, `rating`, `rating_min`, `rating_max`, and `response_count`. Keep the question and patient cohort in the metric definition. Import the aggregate and its provenance. Keep review text and patient identifiers with the original source.

Patient experience receives up to **1 km** or **2 minutes** of additional ranking credit only when all eligible branches in an evidence tier have the same source, metric, scale and reporting window, with at least two comparable branches. The defaults require 30 responses and a reporting end within 365 days. The normalized score shrinks toward the scale midpoint with weight equivalent to 20 responses. Those constants are demonstration choices. Sparse or incompatible profiles remain visible and the tier keeps its existing ranking. No authorized comparable rating export was supplied for the current snapshot. The prepared branch-name-and-address search links work independently.
