# Patient ratings and branch verification pilot

**November 21, 2026.** The pilot used recommendations from the real prepared snapshot. It matched review pages by branch name, street address and PIN where published, then checked whether the feedback covered the recommended service site. Public source links are suitable for readers to inspect. The review copy and third-party aggregates stayed out of the pipeline.

## Measured ranking results

The replay used confirmed results, the balanced policy, demand spreading off, and straight-line distances with TravelTime off. The baseline temporarily set only the patient-experience caps to zero. The second run restored the normal caps. All ranks shown below stayed the same. Eligible counts come from the full search radius before applying the display limit.

| Search and origin | Eligible branches | Usable ratings | Rank with ratings off and with the normal policy |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Jaipur dialysis, city lookup | 1 | 0 | Soni Hospital (1 → 1) |
| Delhi dialysis, 28.642, 77.209 | 1 | 0 | Delhi Heart & Lungs Institute (1 → 1) |
| Jaipur blood bank, city lookup | 43 | 0 | Santokba (1 → 1), S.K. Soni (2 → 2), Pushpa Devi Memorial (3 → 3), Haribaksh Kanwatia (4 → 4), Military Hospital Jaipur (5 → 5) |
| Delhi blood bank, 28.5684, 77.2083, 0.28 km test radius | 2 | 0 | AIIMS Main (1 → 1), Safdarjung (2 → 2) |

Santokba led the Jaipur blood-bank search because it was the one eligible candidate in the dated evidence tier. The other 42 candidates had undated evidence. The JLN Marg Soni Hospital review page belongs to the dialysis hospital and cannot be used for the separately listed S.K. Soni blood bank in north Jaipur. [Soni branch contact](https://www.sonihospitals.com/contact-us/) · [S.K. Soni Blood Bank](https://www.sonihospitals.com/s-k-soni-blood-bank/)

In a **fictional sensitivity test** on the two real Delhi candidates, surveys with the same questions, period, and 100 responses each produced prepared credit of 0.25 km for AIIMS Main and 0.75 km for Safdarjung. The 0.50 km credit difference exceeded the 0.055 km difference in straight-line distance, so Safdarjung moved from second to first. Expanding the test radius to 0.50 km added the AIIMS Cardio Neuro centre with no survey, which withheld review credit for the whole tier and restored AIIMS Main to first. These inputs were kept in memory. They say nothing about those facilities' patient experience.

At the normal 50 km radius the same Delhi coordinates yielded 240 candidates and zero usable ratings. The broader Delhi lookup also exposed a location defect. The saved eRaktKosh source puts Deep Blood Centre (`eraktkosh:283575`) at 28.61288, 77.16731 while its address says Faridkot, Punjab. Its missing PIN bypassed the postal check and the point passed the India-wide bounds check, so the city-based Delhi lookup placed it about 3.74 km away. This is a prepared-location review item. The pilot used the two independently verified Delhi sites for the comparison. [Faridkot district geography](https://faridkot.nic.in/about-district/)

The [follow-up location audit](LOCATION_AUDIT.md) documents the pipeline hold and the other coordinate conflicts.
