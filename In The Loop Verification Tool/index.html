    A quick guide

    1. Import a spreadsheet. Include a screenshot path, its original page URL, and the claims to check.
    2. Compare the evidence. Inspect the screenshot, check each claim, and add an optional note.
    3. Make the call. Choose incorrect, inconclusive, or correct. You can revisit or clear any decision.
    4. Export your reviews. Download the original data with review status, notes, and timestamps. Reimport it to continue elsewhere.

    Each queue has its own page size and page controls. Only one queue opens at a time. Inconclusive counts as reviewed.

    Reviews save in this browser. Export a copy before clearing browser data. Attached local screenshots must be reattached after a refresh. The spreadsheet and reviews stay in your browser.
